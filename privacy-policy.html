3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the App's features
  • To publish content to your social accounts when you request it
  • To send you important notifications about your account
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do NOT use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. TikTok API Data Handling

Our use of TikTok API data complies with TikTok's API Terms of Service. Specifically:

  • We only request permissions necessary to provide the service
  • We do not store TikTok user data beyond what is needed for the immediate task
  • Access tokens are encrypted and stored securely
  • We revoke access immediately upon account disconnection
  • We do not share TikTok user data with any third party

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Hosting providers (Railway/AWS) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements
  • AI providers: OpenAI / Anthropic for script generation (only your content prompts are sent, never personal data)
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our legal rights

6. Data Security

We protect your data using:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest
  • Secure, isolated database environments
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls — only authorized team members can access your data

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Revoke consent: Disconnect your TikTok account at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@viralreelsapp.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for the App to function (session management, authentication). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, please contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside Morocco (e.g., on cloud servers in Europe or the US). In such cases, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data.
